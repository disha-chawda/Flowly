# FLOWLY | Student's Pulse

## 1. Project Overview

FLOWLY is an intelligent student life management platform designed to
help students organize and manage the different responsibilities that
compete for their time and attention.

Students often manage academic deadlines, classes, work schedules,
personal responsibilities, career preparation, transportation, and
important messages across many different platforms. FLOWLY aims to bring
these responsibilities into one planning system.

The goal is not simply to provide another to-do list or calendar.
FLOWLY is intended to help students understand what they need to do,
when they should do it, and what they need to prepare for.

The long-term vision is for FLOWLY to become a personalized planning
system that helps students make realistic decisions about their time
while reducing the mental effort required to keep track of everything.

---

## 2. Project Name

**FLOWLY | Student's Pulse**

The name FLOWLY represents the idea of helping a student's different
responsibilities flow together instead of remaining scattered across
different applications and sources of information.

"Student's Pulse" represents the idea of giving students an ongoing
overview of what is happening in their academic, work, personal, and
career lives.

---

## 3. Problem Statement

Students frequently receive information from many different sources.

Examples include:

- Learning management systems
- Email
- Calendars
- Class schedules
- Work schedules
- Transit applications
- Personal notes
- Task lists
- Career and internship websites

These systems are useful individually, but they generally do not
consider the student's entire schedule together.

As a result, students may:

- Forget deadlines
- Miss important tasks
- Underestimate how long assignments will take
- Schedule too many responsibilities at once
- Leave studying or project work until the last minute
- Fail to account for work or transportation commitments
- Spend significant time manually organizing their schedules
- Experience unnecessary stress from having to remember everything

FLOWLY aims to address this fragmentation by bringing relevant
responsibilities together and turning them into a more understandable
and actionable plan.

---

## 4. Target Users

The primary users of FLOWLY are college and university students who
balance multiple responsibilities.

FLOWLY is particularly intended for students who may simultaneously
manage:

- Courses and assignments
- Exams and projects
- Part-time or full-time work
- Commuting and transportation
- Personal responsibilities
- Career preparation
- Internship applications
- Extracurricular activities

The system should remain useful for students with different schedules
and workloads rather than assuming that every student follows the same
routine.

---

## 5. Core Goal

The core goal of FLOWLY is to help students answer three questions:

1. **What do I need to do?**
2. **When should I do it?**
3. **What do I need to prepare for?**

FLOWLY should transform scattered responsibilities into a clear,
manageable plan.

The system should help students understand their workload rather than
simply displaying a larger collection of tasks.

---

## 6. Core Product Idea

FLOWLY combines tasks, deadlines, commitments, and available time into
one planning system.

Instead of requiring the student to manually decide how every
responsibility fits into their schedule, FLOWLY should progressively
help organize that information.

For example, a student may have:

- A programming assignment due tomorrow
- A work shift in the evening
- A class in the afternoon
- A personal appointment
- An internship application deadline later in the week

FLOWLY should eventually be able to consider these responsibilities
together and help the student determine what should be done first and
when there is realistic time to complete it.

---

## 7. FLOWLY Is More Than a To-Do List

A traditional to-do list primarily answers:

> "What tasks do I have?"

FLOWLY aims to answer a broader question:

> "Given everything happening in my life, what should I focus on
> and when can I realistically do it?"

The long-term system should consider factors such as:

- Deadlines
- Task priority
- Estimated task duration
- Fixed commitments
- Available time
- Workload
- Transportation
- Academic schedules
- Personal responsibilities
- Career deadlines
- Individual planning patterns

This distinction is central to the product vision.

---

## 8. Long-Term Vision

The long-term vision for FLOWLY is an intelligent, personalized
student planning platform that can connect information from the
different systems students already use.

Potential integrations include:

- Google Calendar
- Canvas or other learning management systems
- Email
- Transit services
- Work schedules
- Mobile notifications
- Career and internship tracking

Rather than forcing students to manually copy information between
different applications, FLOWLY could eventually retrieve relevant
information and incorporate it into the student's planning system.

---

## 9. Intelligent Planning

Intelligent planning is a major part of FLOWLY's long-term vision.

The initial version will use simple, deterministic planning rules.
This provides a reliable foundation before introducing more advanced
intelligence.

Future versions may incorporate machine learning or other intelligent
methods to improve planning.

Potential future capabilities include:

- Predicting how long a student may need to complete a task
- Identifying periods of high workload
- Predicting when a student may be at risk of falling behind
- Learning individual planning patterns
- Recommending when to begin studying
- Personalizing task prioritization
- Generating more realistic schedules
- Adjusting recommendations based on previous behavior

The goal is not to add AI simply for the sake of using AI. Intelligence
should improve the usefulness and personalization of the planning
system.

---

## 10. Transportation and Real-World Constraints

A future version of FLOWLY may incorporate transportation information.

For students who commute, transportation can directly affect when they
can realistically complete activities.

For example, FLOWLY could eventually consider:

- Bus or train schedules
- Delays
- Travel time
- Recommended departure times
- Time required to reach campus or work

This would allow planning to account for real-world constraints rather
than treating the student's day as if all available time were equally
usable.

---

## 11. Career and Internship Planning

Career preparation is another area that can eventually become part of
FLOWLY.

Students may need to manage:

- Internship applications
- Application deadlines
- Resume preparation
- Cover letters
- Interview preparation
- Networking activities
- Technical interview preparation

FLOWLY could eventually incorporate these responsibilities into the
same planning system as academic and personal tasks.

This would allow career preparation to become part of a student's
overall schedule rather than something managed separately.

---

## 12. Personalization

FLOWLY should eventually adapt to the individual student.

Different students have different:

- Schedules
- Workloads
- Available hours
- Study habits
- Task completion patterns
- Priorities
- Commitments
- Planning preferences

Future versions should use information about the student's own
behavior and preferences to provide more realistic recommendations.

Personalization should improve the student's ability to make decisions
without removing the student's control over their schedule.

---

## 13. Privacy and User Control

Because FLOWLY may eventually interact with sensitive student
information, privacy and user control are important parts of the
long-term vision.

Future integrations should follow principles such as:

- Only requesting necessary permissions
- Giving students control over connected services
- Clearly communicating what information is being used
- Avoiding unnecessary collection of personal information
- Allowing students to disconnect integrations
- Protecting stored information

The system should help students organize their information without
requiring unnecessary access to their personal accounts.

---

## 14. MVP Philosophy

FLOWLY will be developed incrementally.

The first version will intentionally focus on a small set of core
capabilities:

- Manual task management
- Task deadlines
- Estimated task duration
- Priority
- Categories
- Manual commitments
- Today's tasks
- Upcoming tasks
- Workload summaries
- Basic daily planning
- Persistent storage

The MVP will not attempt to implement every part of the long-term
vision.

This approach allows the core planning experience to be built,
tested, and improved before introducing external integrations and
machine learning.

---

## 15. Development Direction

FLOWLY is intended to evolve through several stages.

### Stage 1: Core Planning MVP

Build the basic task and commitment management system and provide a
simple daily planning experience.

### Stage 2: Calendar Integration

Connect FLOWLY with calendar information so that scheduled events can
be incorporated automatically.

### Stage 3: Academic Integration

Integrate with learning management systems such as Canvas to reduce
manual entry of academic deadlines and responsibilities.

### Stage 4: Email Integration

Allow relevant information from emails to be identified and
incorporated into the student's planning system.

### Stage 5: Transportation Integration

Incorporate travel time, transit schedules, and delays into planning.

### Stage 6: Notifications

Introduce useful reminders through appropriate notification channels.

### Stage 7: Intelligent Planning

Introduce more advanced planning and personalization methods,
potentially including machine learning.

### Stage 8: Personal Student Assistant

Develop a broader intelligent assistant capable of helping students
understand their responsibilities, workload, and upcoming needs.

---

## 16. Success Criteria

FLOWLY should ultimately be successful if it helps students:

- Spend less time manually organizing responsibilities
- Miss fewer deadlines
- Better understand their workload
- Make more realistic plans
- Prepare earlier for important deadlines
- Recognize scheduling conflicts
- Reduce unnecessary last-minute work
- Understand what they should focus on next

The most important measure of FLOWLY's usefulness is whether it helps
a student make better decisions about their time.
